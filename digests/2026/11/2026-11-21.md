## NVIDIA/TensorRT-LLM: v1.3.0

- Breaking: The TensorRT backend was removed in favor of PyTorch as the only backend, eliminating tools including trtllm-build and BuildConfig.
- Breaking: The legacy C++ Executor, runtime APIs, TRTLLMSampler, TensorRT-engine Triton backend models, and two-model speculative decoding were removed.
- Feature: Added preview support for NVIDIA Rubin (SM107) alongside new model support for DeepSeek-V4, Kimi K3, and Qwen3.5.
- Feature: Introduced VisualGen, a vertical solution to serve image and video generation models through trtllm-serve.
- Perf: Added model-specific kernel fusions for DeepSeek-V4 and Qwen3.5, reduced host overhead with async preprocessing, and added locality domain localization for NVFP4 MoE on Rubin.

Source: [v1.3.0](https://github.com/NVIDIA/TensorRT-LLM/releases/tag/v1.3.0)
