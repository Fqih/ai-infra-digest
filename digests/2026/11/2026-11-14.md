## huggingface/transformers: Release v5.23.0

- Breaking: The `ep_router` expert-parallel style was removed, `[audio]` dropped librosa and phonemizer, and exporter configurations replaced `output_path` with `save_pretrained`.
- Feature: New model additions include Inkling2, JEV, LFM3, Dots 3 Note Preview, LFM3-VL, IQuestCoder, and InternLM3.
- Feature: The exporters framework introduced `ExportedModel` to export, share to the Hub, and execute models across Dynamo, ONNX Runtime, OpenVINO, and ExecuTorch backends.
